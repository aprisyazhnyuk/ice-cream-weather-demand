03 · Beyond the calendar

Summer explains the pattern. Extra warmth tests the response.

After removing each market’s normal month-of-year cycle, positive values mean unusually warm months still coincide with unusually high interest.

What stands out

Johannesburg and Sydney react to the weather, not just the season.

Their anomaly correlations both exceed 0.61. Moscow and Rome, by contrast, look more calendar-led over this five-year window.

Moscow focus

In Central Russia, the craving arrives before the real heat.

Moscow’s scoop point is 14.6°C—second only to Toronto. Search interest peaks in June, but warmer-than-usual months add little once the regular seasonal cycle is removed.

Method & caveats

A signal of curiosity, not a receipt.

Google Trends is a normalized search-interest index, used here as a demand-intent proxy—not sales volume. Each market is analyzed within its own 0–100 scale.

The scoop point comes from an increasing isotonic fit of twelve monthly climatology points. Correlations are descriptive and do not establish causation.

Weather is ERA5-Land at city-centre coordinates. Search geographies are administrative regions; London uses England as the narrowest supported fallback.
